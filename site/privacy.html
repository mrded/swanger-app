Privacy Policy

Swanger does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app reads OpenAPI files from your Mac and never sends them — or anything else — over the network. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, and no accounts.

Try it drafts (the JSON payloads you type) are saved locally in your Mac's preferences (UserDefaults) and never leave your device.
